      Mix & master

      Voxloop Studio measures both tracks and decides every setting. Uses the corrected vocal if you ran pitch correction.

      Picks a production flavor for the whole mix (for example brighter, drier, or more punchy).
      How hard the polish pushes clarity, warmth, and evening of the vocal.
      A soft wash that swells after each line, so the song keeps breathing when the singer stops instead of going dead. Its length and level are calculated from your beat and vocal.
      A quiet layer under the voice that makes it sound sung twice: a natural double, one soft echo that never repeats, and a faint harmony. Every setting is calculated from your beat and vocal.
      Turns down harsh "s" sounds and a pushed, edgy tone, only while they are too sharp, so the vocal stays smooth through tuning and mastering. Raise it for a bright or strained voice.
      Gently balances drums and tone on the beat only where it needs help.
      Optional. If the beat feels empty, adds simple supportive layers (kick, hats, bass, pad, piano, strings) locked to the song tempo and key. Mixed under the real beat.
      How loud the voice sits against the beat: closer, balanced, or more forward.
      Optional. Leave blank to use the default file name.
      Brings out the piano, keys, guitars, pads and strings in your beat (and the snare and hats) so you can hear them under the voice, like a studio mix. It lifts them just enough to sit a bit under the vocal, mostly while you sing. Kick and bass are not touched. If they are already audible, nothing changes.
